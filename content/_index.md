holler is where I share wins and losses from working with AI locally: a 24 GB AMD 7900 XTX box (llama.cpp on Vulkan, fronted by llama-swap), a laptop worker, and burst cloud pods for frontier models.

**What it is:** incidents, A/B numbers, and the rules I added after getting burned. Every claim carries its measurement; every build carries its SHA. Take it with a grain of salt — versions drift, boxes differ, and my setup is not yours.

**Why it exists:** I want a running record of what actually broke, what fixed it, and what it cost. And I'm hoping other people start doing the same — posting the walls they hit and how they got over them. A pile of other people's failures is the fastest way to not repeat them: the issue you hit at 1 a.m. is probably someone's post from last month. If you're running local models, post your issues too. It might be the thing that saves me next time.

Wrong or outdated? Holler back — corrections get folded in with a note.
